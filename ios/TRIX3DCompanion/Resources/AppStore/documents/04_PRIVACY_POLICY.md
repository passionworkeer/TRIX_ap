## 1. Introduction

TRIX 3D Companion ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**By using TRIX 3D Companion, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.**

### Our Commitment
- We collect only the information necessary to provide our services
- We never sell your personal information to third parties
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
- You have full control over your data and account

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information**
- **Email address**: Required for account creation and authentication
- **Username**: Display name within the app
- **Profile photo**: Optional, for personalization
- **Apple Sign In ID**: If you choose to sign in with Apple

**Study Data**
- Study session duration and timestamps
- Points earned and redeemed
- Study goals and achievements
- Learning statistics and progress

**User Generated Content**
- Chat messages (text, voice, images)
- Photo snapshots
- Location information (when explicitly shared)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Device Information**
- Device model and operating system version
- Unique device identifier (for analytics only)
- Mobile network information
- App version and build number

**Usage Information**
- Features used within the app
- Time spent in the app
- Crash reports and performance data
- In-app purchase history

**Location Information**
- Precise location: Only when you explicitly enable location sharing
- Approximate location: For discovering nearby study spots
- We never collect location data without your explicit consent

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- **We do not collect**:
  - Contact lists or address books
  - Phone numbers or SMS history
  - Browser history
  - Files or documents from your device
  - Voice recordings (only voice messages you intentionally send)

- To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

- To protect the rights and safety of our users

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and **do not sell** your personal information.

### 4.1 When We May Share Information

**With Your Consent**
- When you explicitly authorize sharing (e.g., sharing location with friends)

**Service Providers**
- Cloud hosting services (for data storage)
- Analytics services (for app performance tracking)
- Payment processors (for in-app purchases)
- Sign-in services (Apple, WeChat)

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

**Legal Requirements**
- To comply with legal processes
- To protect our rights and property
- To protect user safety
- As required by law

### 4.2 What We Never Share

- **We never sell**: Your personal information, email address, or usage data
- **We never rent**: Your data to advertisers or third parties
- **We never share**: Your chat messages with anyone except the intended recipient

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

### 5.1 Data Protection
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Secure Authentication**: Using Apple Sign In and OAuth
- **Keychain Storage**: Sensitive data stored securely in iOS Keychain
- **Secure APIs**: All API communications use HTTPS/TLS

### 5.2 Data Retention
- **Chat Messages**: Stored until you delete them
- **Study Data**: Retained for your historical statistics
- **Account Data**: Kept while your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: All data permanently deleted within 30 days

### 5.3 Your Security Responsibilities
- Keep your Apple ID account secure
- Don't share your password with others
- Log out from shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

You have the following rights regarding your data:

### 6.1 Access and Portability
- Request a copy of your personal data
- View all data we have about you

### 6.2 Correction and Deletion
- Update or correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and all associated data

### 6.3 Control and Objection
- Opt-out of location tracking
- Disable specific features
- Object to data processing (where legally permitted)
